    Sources & limitations

    Useful signals.
    Not promises.

    This is a curated snapshot of public company facts, not a complete market directory. A directory’s hiring flag is unverified; even a board snapshot is historical, not a guarantee of an open role. Dates in company details describe when facts were imported, not when jobs were posted.

    Stage is a documented estimate from available company facts. Unknown facts stay unknown. Location resolution is approximate, and broad country pins do not imply an office at that point.

    Search and filters run in your browser. No profile, CV, email address, applications or private local preferences are published here. No analytics are added. Street map tiles load from CARTO (OpenStreetMap data) when you view the map; company logos and data are self-hosted, so no logo or geocoding service is contacted. The hosting provider may retain ordinary request logs.
