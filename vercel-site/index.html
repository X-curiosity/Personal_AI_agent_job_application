Privacy is the architecture

Your career data lives on your disk

Co-Apply GPT is a local application. There is no account system, no analytics, and no server of ours to breach.

  • SQLite on your machine, permission-locked to your OS user
  • No tokens stored — LinkedIn OIDC tokens are discarded after identity
  • Remote logos and avatars are off until you opt in
  • Gmail reads only exact conversations you bind and consent to
  • No LinkedIn scraping or automation, ever
  • Applications are prepared for your approval — never auto-submitted
