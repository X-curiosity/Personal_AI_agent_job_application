Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who operates Hired

Hired is operated by Xavier Magbi. Privacy and support contact: magxavier19@icloud.com.

Information we process

The public Jobs/Map site processes no account or CV. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary request logs, and map tiles are requested from OpenFreeMap.

If hosted accounts are enabled, we process your email and authentication metadata; your Privacy Notice receipt; CV files; target companies; job links; application stages and notes; and optional LinkedIn basic identity (name, email, profile image, and provider identifiers). CV text is parsed on demand inside the hosted application for deterministic skill comparison. Matched and missing public skill terms may be saved in application notes.

Purposes and legal bases

We use this information to provide the account, storage, matching, export, deletion, and application-preparation features you request (performance of a contract), maintain security and service reliability (legitimate interests), and make optional LinkedIn connections or employer submissions at your direction (consent or your explicit action, as applicable). Do not upload information you are not entitled to use.

Providers and locations

Planned account hosting is Render in Frankfurt. Authentication, Postgres, private file storage, and transactional email use Supabase; the current project is hosted in US East. Vercel hosts the public site and synthetic test forms. LinkedIn processes identity only when you choose its official connection. Provider terms, subprocessors, logs, and backup policies also apply.

The local rule-based auto-apply runner uses no AI model and does not send screenshots or CVs to a model provider. Your CV and answers go to the employer only when you approve that application. Hosted services never silently submit applications.

Retention, export, and deletion

Hosted workspace data is kept until you delete it or your account. You can request a ZIP export of account-linked records and original CVs. Account deletion requires a fresh email code and removes active CV objects, hosted records, and linked identity data. Cross-service cleanup can fail; contact support if this happens. Provider backups may retain deleted data for the period defined by the provider’s then-current backup policy. OAuth state records expire after ten minutes and are single-use.

Security and your choices

Private tables use owner-scoped row-level security and CVs use a non-public bucket with short-lived signed downloads. No safeguard guarantees absolute security. You may use the public explorer without an account or LinkedIn connection. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact the operator to exercise these rights; we aim to respond within 30 days where feasible and within legally required periods.

Changes and incidents

Material changes receive a new version and date. Suspected privacy or security issues can be reported to magxavier19@icloud.com. Where legally required, affected users and authorities will be notified.
